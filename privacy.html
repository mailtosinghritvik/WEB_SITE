Privacy

Privacy notice.

AgentOrgs is a service. This page says what we actually do with personal information. Last updated 30 October 2026.

Who we are

AgentOrgs Inc. builds operating software and AI agents for a business, then runs that work with them. We are in Toronto, Ontario. Privacy questions go to privacy@agentorgs.com.

This website

The site does not use analytics and does not set tracking cookies. “Start a conversation” opens your email app, addressed to us. We only see what you choose to send.

The pages are hosted on GitHub. That host may keep ordinary server logs, such as an IP address and the page requested, in order to deliver the site.

Work we do for a client

A client’s records belong to that client. We use them to build and run the system they asked for, under the agreement and any NDA we have signed. The records are stored where that client operates, in an environment kept separate from other clients.

Anything an agent pulls in waits for a person to approve before it is written into the client’s books. If your information is in a system we run for someone else, ask that business. We will help them answer.

We keep correspondence and the records a job needs for as long as the work continues, and for as long as the agreement says after that.

What we don’t do

We do not sell personal information. We do not buy contact lists. We do not take payment on this website, and we do not store card numbers. We are paid as a service, under the agreement with the client.

Asking us

Email privacy@agentorgs.com to see what we hold about you, to correct it, or to ask us to delete it. In Canada you can also contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner at priv.gc.ca.

If this notice changes, the new version will be on this page, with a new date.
